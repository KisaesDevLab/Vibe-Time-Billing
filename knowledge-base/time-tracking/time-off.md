# Time off — balances, requests, and sick time

Your PTO / Vacation, Sick, and Comp-time balances live on the **Time off** page (`/time-off`), along with the request form and your request history.

## Your balances

Three cards show each bank's current balance, with accrued and used hours underneath. Balances accrue automatically per your firm's accrual policy (fixed hours per pay period, hours-worked-based, or an annual grant — ask your admin which applies to you). A negative balance shows in red.

## Requesting time off

1. Open **Time off** and pick a **Type** (PTO / Vacation, Sick, Comp time, or Unpaid leave).
2. Set the **First day** and **Last day**. Weekdays in the range are prefilled with hours; edit any day (set a half day, zero out a day you'll work).
3. The form shows your projected balance after the request — you can go negative, it just warns.
4. Add an optional note and click **Request**.

Approvers (partners/managers) are notified. When your request is decided you get a notification; an **approved** request automatically creates the time entries on the internal Administrative engagement and deducts your balance — nothing else to log.

Pending requests can be **cancelled** from the list.

## Same-day sick time

You don't need a request for unplanned absences: log time on the **Time** page against the internal **Administrative time** engagement with the **Sick leave** work code (or **PTO / Vacation**, **Comp time used**, **Unpaid leave**). The entry deducts from the matching balance the moment it's saved; if it overdraws, the save still succeeds with a warning.

## Tips

- Editing or deleting a PTO/Sick entry automatically corrects your balance — usage is computed from the entries themselves.
- Once a payroll period is **locked**, entries dated inside it can't be added or changed ("locked payroll period" error). Ask whoever runs payroll to unlock if a correction is needed.
- Approving managers see an **Approvals** tab on the same page listing pending requests.
