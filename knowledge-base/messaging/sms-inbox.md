# Messages → SMS (two-way texting)

The SMS tab is the firm's shared texting inbox. Every conversation is one client number on one firm line.

## Reading

- **Filters**: Unread · Unassigned (no client linked) · Needs triage (a number shared by several contacts) · Mine (assigned to you) · All. Search matches contact name, number, or message text.
- Opening a conversation marks it read after a moment. Use **Mark unread** from the ⋯ menu to keep it in the Unread view.
- Unread texts also count in the **Messages** nav badge and the Dashboard "Needs attention" card. Desktop notifications (desktop app or browser) can be turned on under Account → Notifications; you're notified for texts assigned to you or unassigned.

## Who is texting

Vibe links a conversation to a client automatically: from the reminder/booking/request the client is replying to, or by matching the number to a contact. One match links the client and suggests the client's only open engagement (a _Suggested_ chip — your first reply confirms it). Several matches show a **Needs triage** prompt; pick the right person or **Link to client…** for anything else (optionally saving the number on the contact). Manual links are never overridden.

## Replying

- Replies go out from the line the client texted. **New text** (inbox, client SMS tab, engagement panel, or a person record) lets you pick the line when the firm has several.
- **Templates** insert firm or personal quick replies with `{client_first}`, `{engagement_name}`, `{staff_first}`, `{firm}` filled in.
- The counter shows characters, encoding, and how many SMS segments the carrier will bill.
- A warning appears if the draft looks like it contains an SSN, EIN, card, routing, or account number, or a date of birth. Texts aren't encrypted end-to-end; keep that data in the portal.
- Blocked states: **Opted out** (the client texted STOP — only they can undo it with START), **No consent on file** (record verbal consent, or wait for them to text first), **10DLC not registered** (admin setup), **Closed** (reopen to reply).

## Appointment replies

When a reminder went out from Vibe, a reply of **C / Y / CONFIRM** confirms the appointment and gets an automatic acknowledgement; **R / RESCHEDULE** opens a reschedule request and notifies the assignee (or the appointment lead) and stays unread.

## Closing and time

**Close** ends a thread (a new inbound text reopens it). On close — or any time from the ⋯ menu — **Create time entry** prefills the client, engagement, default work code, and an editable duration estimate.

## Attachments

Pictures and PDFs a client texts land in **Document Intake** with AI naming; the thumbnail in the thread opens the intake session.
