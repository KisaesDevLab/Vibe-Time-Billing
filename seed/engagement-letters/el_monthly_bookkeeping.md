# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms our understanding of the bookkeeping services we will provide to {{client.name}} (the "Company") on an ongoing monthly basis, beginning {{date engagement.start_date "long"}}.

## Scope of services

We will provide the following services each month:

- Recording of transactions in the Company's accounting system based on documentation you provide
- Bank account reconciliation through month-end
- Credit card account reconciliation through month-end
- Monthly close including review of revenue, expense, and balance sheet activity
- Generation of standard monthly financial reports (balance sheet, income statement, cash flow summary)
- Routine client communication regarding bookkeeping matters

{{engagement.scope_definition}}

## What is included in the monthly retainer

The monthly retainer covers up to **{{engagement.budget_hours}}** of in-scope work per month. Specific services covered:

- Transaction entry and categorization
- Bank and credit card reconciliations
- Monthly close procedures
- Standard month-end reports
- Up to 30 minutes of phone or email communication per month

## What is NOT included

The following services are out-of-scope and billed separately at our standard hourly rates:

- Year-end tax preparation (covered under separate engagement)
- Sales tax return preparation
- Payroll processing
- Bookkeeping cleanup for prior periods
- Cash flow forecasting and budgeting
- Custom reporting beyond standard packages
- Audit, review, or compilation services
- Responses to taxing-authority notices
- Implementation of new accounting systems
- Special projects, advisory consultations, or financial analysis

Out-of-scope work will be quoted and approved before commencement when feasible.

## Fees

Our monthly fee is **{{engagement.fee_amount}}**, billed in advance on the first business day of each month under our {{engagement.fee_structure}} arrangement. Payment is due net {{client.terms_days}} days from invoice date.

If actual hours in any month exceed the included scope, overage hours are billed at the end of the month at our standard rates. We will notify you when an overage is approaching so you can adjust scope or budget accordingly.

The monthly fee will be reviewed annually and may be adjusted with 30 days written notice.

## Information and access

The Company agrees to:

- Provide bank and credit card statements within 5 business days of month-end
- Provide receipts, invoices, and documentation for all transactions
- Designate a primary contact for bookkeeping questions
- Respond to our information requests within 5 business days

Delays in receiving information may delay our deliverables; we will not be responsible for missed deadlines caused by client information delays.

## Disclosures

**Bookkeeping is not assurance.** Our bookkeeping services are not an audit, review, or compilation. We will not provide any assurance regarding the financial statements. The Company is responsible for the accuracy of the financial records.

**Reliance on information.** We rely on the information you provide. Material misstatements in source documents will result in misstated financials and may require additional work to correct.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Upon termination, we will deliver the Company's books to you in a usable format (e.g., QuickBooks file, Excel exports). Termination does not relieve the Company of obligations for services rendered through the termination date.

**Data retention.** We retain client records in accordance with applicable professional standards and our Written Information Security Plan (WISP). Records may be destroyed after the retention period unless legal hold applies.
