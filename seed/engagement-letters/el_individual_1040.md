# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms the terms of our engagement to prepare your individual income tax return for the {{engagement.name}} tax year and outlines the nature and limitations of the services we will provide.

## Scope of services

We will prepare your federal and state individual income tax returns based on information you provide. Specifically, we will:

- Prepare Form 1040 and accompanying schedules
- Prepare applicable state tax return(s)
- Electronically file the returns on your behalf upon your authorization
- Respond to routine questions arising during preparation

{{engagement.scope_definition}}

## Information you will provide

You agree to provide us with all the information necessary to prepare complete and accurate returns. This includes, without limitation:

- W-2s, 1099s, and other income statements
- Records of deductible expenses
- Prior-year tax returns (if you are a new client)
- Documentation of significant transactions during the year

We rely on the information you provide and do not audit or verify it. If information you provide proves to be materially incorrect, we may need to amend the return, which is billed separately.

## Fees

Our fee for these services is **{{engagement.fee_amount}}**, billed under a {{engagement.fee_structure}} arrangement. Payment is due net {{client.terms_days}} days from invoice date.

Additional services beyond the scope above — including amended returns, responses to taxing-authority notices, tax planning, and audit support — are billed separately at our standard hourly rates. We will inform you in advance before commencing any out-of-scope work.

## Disclosures

**Scope creep.** Work outside the agreed scope of this engagement will be billed separately at our standard hourly rates. Examples include responding to IRS notices, amended returns, and any work arising from client-provided information that proves materially incorrect.

**Information accuracy.** We rely on information provided by the client. We do not audit or verify this information unless specifically engaged to do so. Information that proves materially incorrect may require additional work, billed separately.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Termination does not relieve the client of obligations for services rendered through the termination date.

**Data retention.** We retain client records in accordance with applicable professional standards and our Written Information Security Plan (WISP). Records may be destroyed after the retention period unless legal hold applies.

**Standards.** This engagement will be conducted in accordance with Statements on Standards for Tax Services issued by the AICPA. We are not engaged to provide attest services with respect to your financial information.
