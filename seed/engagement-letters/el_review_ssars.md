# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms our understanding of the terms of our engagement to review the financial statements of {{client.name}} (the "Company") for the period ending {{date engagement.end_date "long"}}.

## Objective and scope

Our engagement is to perform a review of the financial statements, which include the balance sheet, statements of income, retained earnings, and cash flows for the period then ended, and the related notes.

The objective of a review is to obtain limited assurance as a basis for reporting whether we are aware of any material modifications that should be made to the financial statements for them to be in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (U.S. GAAP).

{{engagement.scope_definition}}

## Review procedures and standards

We will conduct our review in accordance with Statements on Standards for Accounting and Review Services (SSARS) issued by the AICPA's Accounting and Review Services Committee.

A review consists primarily of:

- Inquiries of Company personnel
- Analytical procedures applied to financial data
- Limited additional procedures we deem necessary

A review is substantially less in scope than an audit conducted in accordance with U.S. GAAS, the objective of which is the expression of an opinion regarding the financial statements as a whole. Accordingly, we will not express an opinion on the financial statements.

A review does not provide assurance regarding internal control or identify reportable conditions or material weaknesses. We are not engaged to detect fraud or noncompliance with laws and regulations.

## Management responsibilities

Management is responsible for:

- The fair presentation of the financial statements in accordance with U.S. GAAP
- Establishing and maintaining effective internal control over financial reporting
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining controls relevant to the preparation and fair presentation of financial statements that are free from material misstatement (whether due to fraud or error)
- Providing us with access to all financial records and related information
- Providing a representation letter signed by appropriate members of management

## Fees

## Disclosures

**Limited assurance.** Because of the inherent limitations of a review engagement, the procedures we perform may not detect all material misstatements that may exist.

**No audit opinion.** We will not express an opinion or any form of assurance other than as described.

**Workpaper retention.** Review workpapers are the property of {{firm.name}} and retained per our Written Information Security Plan (WISP) and applicable professional standards.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Termination does not relieve the Company of obligations for services rendered through the termination date.
