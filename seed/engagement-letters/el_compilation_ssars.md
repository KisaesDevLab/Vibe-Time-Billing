# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms our understanding of the terms of our engagement to compile the financial statements of {{client.name}} (the "Company") for the period ending {{date engagement.end_date "long"}}.

## Objective and scope

Our engagement is to apply accounting and financial reporting expertise to assist management in the presentation of the Company's financial statements. We will compile, from information you provide, the balance sheet, statements of income, retained earnings, and cash flows for the period then ended, and the related notes.

A compilation engagement does not contemplate performing inquiry, analytical procedures, or other procedures ordinarily performed in a review. We will not provide any assurance on the financial statements.

{{engagement.scope_definition}}

## Compilation standards

We will conduct our compilation in accordance with Statements on Standards for Accounting and Review Services (SSARS) issued by the AICPA's Accounting and Review Services Committee. Our compilation report will state that we have not audited or reviewed the financial statements and accordingly do not express an opinion, a conclusion, nor provide any assurance on them.

## Management responsibilities

Management is responsible for:

- The fair presentation of the financial statements in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework
- The design, implementation, and maintenance of internal control relevant to the preparation and fair presentation of financial statements
- Preventing and detecting fraud
- Ensuring compliance with laws and regulations
- The accuracy and completeness of records, documents, explanations, and other information provided to us
- Making all financial records and related information available to us

## Fees

Our fee for this engagement is **{{engagement.fee_amount}}**, billed under our {{engagement.fee_structure}} arrangement, due net {{client.terms_days}} days from invoice date.

If management requires changes to the financial statements after compilation work has commenced, or if additional schedules are required, those services are billed separately at our standard hourly rates.

## Disclosures

**No assurance.** A compilation is the lowest level of service under SSARS. We provide no assurance — limited or otherwise — on the financial statements. If you require assurance, a review or audit engagement is appropriate; we are happy to discuss those options.

**Limited use.** The financial statements may be used only by the Company and parties to whom the Company chooses to provide them. Third parties relying on the financial statements should be informed that they were compiled without assurance.

**Workpaper retention.** Compilation workpapers are the property of {{firm.name}} and retained per our Written Information Security Plan (WISP) and applicable professional standards.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice.
