# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms our understanding of the payroll services we will provide to {{client.name}} (the "Company") on an ongoing basis, beginning {{date engagement.start_date "long"}}.

## Scope of services

We will provide the following payroll services:

- Payroll processing on the Company's regular pay schedule
- Calculation of gross wages, withholdings, and net pay
- Direct deposit setup and processing
- Calculation and remittance of federal and state payroll taxes
- Federal payroll tax filings (Forms 941, 940)
- State payroll tax filings (state withholding, unemployment)
- Year-end W-2 and applicable 1099 processing
- New hire reporting to applicable state agencies
- Maintenance of employee earnings records

## What is included in the monthly retainer

The monthly retainer covers payroll processing for up to **{{engagement.budget_hours}} employees** per pay period. Specific services covered:

- Regular payroll processing
- Tax calculations and remittance
- Quarterly and annual tax filings
- Year-end W-2/1099 issuance
- Routine garnishment and deduction administration
- New hire onboarding and termination processing

## What is NOT included

Out-of-scope services billed separately at our standard hourly rates:

- Multi-state expansion setup
- Voluntary benefit plan administration
- 401(k) or other retirement plan administration
- Workers' compensation audit support
- Tax notice resolution beyond routine response
- Custom report development
- Employee handbook or HR consulting
- Litigation support and subpoenas
- Pay-period audits or wage-and-hour analysis

## Fees

Our monthly fee is **{{engagement.fee_amount}}** for up to {{engagement.budget_hours}} employees, billed in advance under our {{engagement.fee_structure}} arrangement. Additional employees beyond the included count are billed at our per-employee rate.

Payment is due net {{client.terms_days}} days from invoice date.

The Company is responsible for payroll funding. We will ACH-debit the Company's operating account on the scheduled funding date. Failed ACH debits are subject to a $50 returned-item fee and may delay payroll.

## Company responsibilities

The Company agrees to:

- Provide accurate and timely time and attendance data
- Notify us of new hires, terminations, and rate changes by the agreed-upon deadline each pay period
- Maintain sufficient funds in the funding account on scheduled funding dates
- Notify us promptly of any payroll-related correspondence from taxing authorities
- Provide quarterly and annual reconciliation information as requested
- Maintain I-9 documentation and other employment records (we do not retain these)

## Disclosures

**Funding responsibility.** The Company remains liable for payroll tax obligations regardless of our processing role. If the Company fails to provide funding, payroll taxes will not be remitted and the Company will be liable for penalties and interest assessed by taxing authorities.

**Information reliance.** We process payroll based on information provided by the Company. We do not audit or verify the underlying time records, employment status determinations, or compensation arrangements.

**Worker classification.** The Company is responsible for proper classification of workers as employees or independent contractors. We will not provide an opinion on classification unless separately engaged.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Upon termination, we will provide year-to-date earnings records and access to historical filings.

**Data retention.** We retain payroll records in accordance with applicable professional standards, IRS requirements (4 years minimum), and our Written Information Security Plan (WISP).
