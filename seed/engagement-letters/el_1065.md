We will prepare the following on behalf of the Partnership:

- Federal Form 1065, U.S. Return of Partnership Income
- Schedule K-1 for each partner
- Applicable state partnership returns and composite returns
- Partner capital account maintenance under the tax-basis method

We require the following:

- General ledger or trial balance for the tax year
- Bank reconciliations through year-end
- Partner contribution and distribution records
- Section 754 election documentation if applicable

- Prior-year federal and state returns (if new client)
- Partner information necessary for K-1 preparation

The Partnership is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of this information.

## Disclosures

**Scope creep.** Out-of-scope services will be billed separately. Examples include tax planning, amended returns, IRS or state notice responses, partnership audit elections, §754 step-up calculations, and complex allocation studies under §704(b)/(c).

**Partnership representative.** The Partnership has designated a Partnership Representative under the Bipartisan Budget Act audit regime. The Partnership Representative has authority to bind all partners with respect to federal partnership audits. We are not engaged to advise on this designation unless specifically engaged.

**Information accuracy.** We rely on the Partnership's books and records and on information provided by partners. We do not audit or verify it.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Termination does not relieve the Partnership of obligations for services rendered through the termination date.

**Data retention.** We retain client records in accordance with applicable professional standards and our Written Information Security Plan (WISP). Records may be destroyed after the retention period unless legal hold applies.

**Professional standards.** This engagement is conducted in accordance with Statements on Standards for Tax Services issued by the AICPA.
