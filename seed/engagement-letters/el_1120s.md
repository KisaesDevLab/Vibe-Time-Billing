# Engagement Letter — {{engagement.name}}

Dear {{client.billing_contact_name}},

This letter confirms our understanding of the services we will provide to {{client.name}} (the "Company") for the {{engagement.name}} tax year.

## Scope of services

We will prepare the following on behalf of the Company:

- Federal Form 1120-S, U.S. Income Tax Return for an S Corporation
- Schedule K-1 for each shareholder
- Applicable state S-corporation returns
- State composite returns where required and elected
- Electronic filing of returns upon authorization

{{engagement.scope_definition}}

## Information the Company will provide

We require the following to complete the engagement:

- General ledger or trial balance for the tax year
- Bank reconciliations through year-end
- Shareholder distribution records and basis worksheets
- Records of significant transactions (asset purchases/sales, debt activity)
- Prior-year federal and state returns (if new client)
- Shareholder information necessary to prepare K-1s

The Company is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of this information. We do not audit or verify it.

## Fees and milestones

Our fee for this engagement is **{{engagement.fee_amount}}**, structured as follows under our {{engagement.fee_structure}} arrangement:

- 50% retainer due upon execution of this engagement letter
- 50% due upon final filing of the federal return

Additional services are billed separately at our standard hourly rates. We will provide written estimates before commencing out-of-scope work.

## Disclosures

**Scope creep.** Work outside the scope above will be billed separately. This includes (but is not limited to) tax planning, amended returns, IRS or state notice responses, S-corporation reasonable-compensation analysis beyond routine review, and ownership-change studies.

**Information accuracy.** We rely on the Company's books and records. If information proves materially incorrect, additional work may be required and billed separately.

**Reasonable compensation.** S-corporation shareholder-officers are required to receive reasonable compensation for services performed. We will review the Company's officer compensation as part of routine preparation, but a comprehensive reasonable-compensation study is out of scope unless separately engaged.

**Termination.** Either party may terminate this engagement upon 30 days written notice. Termination does not relieve the Company of obligations for services rendered through the termination date.

**Data retention.** We retain client records in accordance with applicable professional standards and our Written Information Security Plan (WISP). Records may be destroyed after the retention period unless legal hold applies.

**Professional standards.** This engagement is conducted in accordance with Statements on Standards for Tax Services issued by the AICPA. We are not engaged to provide attest services.
