# From proposal to signed engagement, in one place

Win the work with a proposal that looks like your firm, capture the signature in the client's browser, and turn it into a live, fully configured engagement — without re-keying a thing.

## Proposals that close

- **A real editor** — build proposals from content blocks: rich text, pricing tables, and embedded video (YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom).
- **Selectable packages** — present pricing options the client can choose from.
- **Digital signature capture** — the client reviews and signs in the portal; acceptance is tracked and tamper-evident.
- **Recurring-revenue rollup** — see the monthly recurring value a proposal represents before you send it.
- **Renewal uplift** — apply a price increase when rolling an engagement forward.

## Engagements that run themselves

- **Engagement templates** — start new engagements from your firm's standard setups, with the right fee structure and work codes already in place.
- **Lifecycle states** — move engagements through proposed, active, paused, and closed, with the right guardrails at each step.
- **Rollforward wizard** — roll a whole book of engagements into the next year in a guided, multi-step flow: pick the scope, review each engagement, generate the related appointments, and commit.

**Why firms care:** the gap between "client said yes" and "engagement is set up correctly" is where detail gets lost. Vibe PM closes it — the proposal _becomes_ the engagement.
