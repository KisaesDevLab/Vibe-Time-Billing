# Scheduling and booking that fit into your calendar

Let clients book time with the right person, keep everyone's calendar in sync, and stop the back-and-forth of finding a slot.

- **Public booking links** — share a link that lets clients book an appointment through a guided flow, with the appointment types, locations, and availability you define.
- **Appointment types and locations** — configure what can be booked, where, and with whom.
- **Staff availability** — control who's bookable and when.
- **Two-way calendar sync** — staff connect their Google or Outlook calendars so appointments and availability stay accurate in both places.
- **Conflict detection** — bookings respect real availability so you don't get double-booked.
- **Reminders** — automated appointment reminders cut down on no-shows.

**Why firms care:** scheduling friction is lost time on both sides. Booking links and live calendar sync turn "what's a good time?" into a solved problem.
