# Invoices that match the engagement, not a template

Compose invoices from any mix of work, brand them as your firm, and deliver them the way each client prefers — all from one place.

- **Mixed line items** — combine time, fixed fees, milestones, recurring charges, expenses, processing fees, surcharges, and custom lines on a single invoice.
- **Consolidated or separate** — bill a client's multiple engagements on one invoice or keep them apart, per the client's preference.
- **Custom numbering** — invoice numbering schemes that fit your firm, assigned cleanly with no gaps.
- **Adjustable detail** — show a summary, a by-line breakdown, or full time-entry detail.
- **Branded PDFs** — your logo, colors, and terms on every invoice.
- **Email delivery** — send straight from the system through your own mail provider, and resend with one click.
- **Portal read receipts** — see whether a client has actually viewed an invoice in the portal — no tracking pixels, no guesswork.
- **Pay-to-unlock attachments** — gate a deliverable behind payment and release it automatically when the invoice clears.
- **Voids and credit memos** — correct cleanly with a full record.

**Why firms care:** invoicing is the client's most frequent touchpoint with your firm. Vibe PM makes every invoice accurate, on-brand, and easy to pay.
