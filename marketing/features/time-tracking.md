# Time tracking built for how accountants actually work

Capture billable and non-billable time against the right engagement, with the context your billing needs already attached. Entry is fast, consistent across the firm, and structured so nothing slips through to month-end as a surprise.

- **Day, week, and month grids** — review and enter time in the view that fits the moment, with billable and non-billable totals broken out per day.
- **Quick entry** — log an entry in seconds: engagement, work code, hours, description.
- **Description templates per work code** — start from your firm's standard language instead of a blank box, so entries read consistently on the invoice.
- **Billable and in-scope tagging** — mark whether time is billable and, on retainer engagements, whether it falls inside the agreed scope — determined the moment you enter it.
- **Late-entry alerts and lockouts** — get nudged about un-entered time, and enforce a firm-wide cutoff so old time can't quietly reopen a closed period.
- **Entry locking** — once time lands on a finalized bill, it locks, so your billed history stays stable.
- **Transfer between engagements** — move an entry to the right engagement with a full audit trail of the change.
- **Required-field rules** — set the fields your firm insists on so every entry is bill-ready.

**Why firms care:** clean, consistent, in-scope time data is the foundation of accurate realization. Vibe PM front-loads that quality at the point of entry instead of fixing it at billing time.
