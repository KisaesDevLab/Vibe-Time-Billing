# One login per person — even across several entities

Your clients are people, not account numbers. Vibe PM's portal is built around that: one person gets one login, and switches between every entity they're connected to at your firm — no separate accounts, no forgotten passwords.

- **Identity-based access** — a single person can be tied to multiple clients and switch between them from a header picker, each view scoped to that client's data.
- **Sign in by email or text** — a passwordless magic link or a one-time SMS code; the login figures out which from what they type.
- **Invoices and payments** — clients view open and paid invoices, pay by card or ACH, save a payment method once and reuse it across every entity they manage, and download receipts.
- **Secure messaging** — engagement-level messages encrypted at rest with a key your firm controls.
- **Document collection (Requests)** — ask clients for documents in-app; their responses can convert to time-entry suggestions for the assigned staff member.
- **Files with pay-to-unlock** — share deliverables, including ones that unlock the moment the gating invoice is paid.
- **Per-client notification preferences** — each person chooses email or SMS, and which events they hear about.
- **Your brand** — your logo, colors, and domain.
- **Mobile-ready** — works cleanly from a phone, where most clients will open it.

**Why firms care:** the portal that's actually pleasant to use is the one clients log into — which means faster payments, fewer document chases, and a firm that looks as modern as it is.
