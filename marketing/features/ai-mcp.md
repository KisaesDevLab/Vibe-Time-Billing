# AI that stays on your server — and an open door for your agents

Vibe PM puts useful AI right where the work happens, running on a local model by default so your client data doesn't have to leave the building. And for firms building their own automation, it exposes a clean, governed interface for AI agents.

## AI built into the workflow

- **Description suggestions** — get a suggested time-entry description right in the entry form, so notes are fast and consistent.
- **Scope-creep alerts** — surface retainer engagements where work is drifting past the agreed scope, before it eats your margin.
- **Pricing suggestions** — get a data-driven price recommendation on an engagement, with the reasoning behind it.

## Local-first, your choice

- **Runs locally by default** — a local model handles AI features so client data stays on your appliance.
- **Bring your own provider** — optionally connect a cloud AI provider if you'd rather; it's opt-in, per firm.
- **A budget you control** — set a monthly AI spend cap that warns as you approach it and stops before you blow past it.

## An MCP server for your agents

- **A governed interface for AI agents** — Vibe PM ships a Model Context Protocol server that lets AI tools read your data and take actions through a defined set of tools.
- **Scoped, token-based access** — each token is limited to the specific tools it's allowed to use.
- **Everything's audited** — actions taken through the server are recorded like any other change.

**Why firms care:** you get the productivity of AI without surrendering data sovereignty — and if your firm is investing in AI workflows, there's a real, secured way to plug them in.
