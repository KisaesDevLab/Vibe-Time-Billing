# Self-hosted, firm-owned, one flat annual license

Vibe PM runs on hardware you control, as a Docker appliance you can stand up in an afternoon. Your data stays yours, and the pricing doesn't punish you for growing.

- **A Docker appliance** — a single, self-contained deployment that runs on commodity hardware, including both Intel and ARM machines.
- **Three deployment modes** — pick the access model that fits your security posture:
  - **Domain mode** — public web access at your own subdomains.
  - **LAN mode** — available on your local network.
  - **Private mesh mode** — staff and portal access gated behind a private network.
- **Your data on your infrastructure** — no mandatory cloud, no vendor holding your client records.
- **You own the integrations** — your payment processor, your domain, your accounts. The vendor never holds your credentials.
- **One annual license, unlimited everything** — unlimited timekeepers and unlimited clients for a single flat fee. No per-seat meter, no per-client tier.
- **Built-in backup and restore** — scheduled backups to a destination you choose, with a documented recovery procedure.

**Why firms care:** you get modern, hosted-grade software without the trade-offs of SaaS — no data leaving your walls, no surprise per-seat bills, and no dependency on a vendor staying in business to keep your firm running.
