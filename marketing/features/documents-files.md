# A document workspace that knows about your billing

Keep client files organized, control exactly who can see what, and tie document delivery to getting paid — something a generic file share can't do.

- **Private vs. client-visible** — every file has a visibility setting; flip a file between internal-only and client-visible in one click.
- **Folder templates** — apply a consistent folder structure to new clients automatically.
- **Secure share links** — share a file through a protected link gated by a one-time code.
- **Pay-to-unlock deliverables** — hold a deliverable behind an unpaid invoice; it's released to the client automatically the moment the invoice is paid, and the portal shows a clear "unlock to view" prompt until then.
- **In-app document collection** — request documents from clients in the portal and receive them straight into the workspace.
- **Flexible storage** — keep files on the appliance or connect your own cloud storage bucket.

**Why firms care:** documents and dollars are connected in real practice. Pay-to-unlock turns your deliverables into a gentle, automatic nudge to settle the invoice — no awkward follow-up call required.
