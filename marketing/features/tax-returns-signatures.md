# Tax-return delivery and e-signatures, handled

Track returns through the engagement, deliver them to clients securely, and collect signatures — all without leaving the system or stitching together a separate e-sign tool.

## Tax-return tracking and delivery

- **Return tracking** — follow returns and their sections through the workflow.
- **Tax-payment catalog** — record and surface the payments tied to a return.
- **Secure client delivery** — release a return to the client, in full or scoped, with a cover note; clients open it through a protected, watermarked view.

## Built-in e-signatures

- **Send for signature** — upload a document and route it to one or more signers.
- **Drag-and-drop field placement** — place signature and data fields exactly where they belong on the page.
- **Multi-signer support** — collect signatures from everyone who needs to sign, in order.
- **Audit trail** — every signature event is recorded for your records.

**Why firms care:** delivery and signing are the last, most visible steps of tax season. Doing them inside your practice-management system — securely and on your brand — beats bouncing clients to a third-party portal.
