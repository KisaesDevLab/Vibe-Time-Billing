# Get paid faster — through your own merchant account

Connect your own payment processor and let clients pay by card or bank transfer. Then let the system chase what's outstanding so your team doesn't have to.

- **Your processor, your money** — connect your own Stripe account; payments flow through your merchant relationship, not a middleman.
- **Card and ACH** — give clients both options; card data is tokenized, never stored by the app.
- **Auto-apply payments** — apply incoming payments to the oldest invoice automatically, or to a specific one.
- **Partial payments and refunds** — handle real-world payment behavior, with full and partial refunds and credit memos.
- **AR aging** — nightly snapshots bucket receivables by age so you always know what's outstanding.
- **Automated dunning** — a configurable reminder sequence escalates overdue invoices with payment links, and can pause engagements after repeated non-payment.
- **Statements** — generate and send client statements with a direct link to pay.
- **Collection metrics** — track days sales outstanding, collection rate, and average days to pay.

**Why firms care:** faster collections and a shorter AR cycle, with none of your clients' payment data — or your processing fees — passing through a vendor in the middle.
