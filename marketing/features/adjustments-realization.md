# Realization that survives down to the individual timekeeper

When a partner writes an engagement up or down, the question every firm struggles with is _whose_ realization absorbs it. Most tools spread the hit evenly and call it a day. Vibe PM lets you decide — precisely — and keeps the result at the individual-timekeeper grain.

- **Three adjustment methods** — adjust the rate, the hours, or the engagement fee.
- **Six allocation methods** — choose how the adjustment lands:
  - **Specific entries** — adjust only the entries you name.
  - **Pro-rata by value** — spread proportionally to each timekeeper's billable amount.
  - **Pro-rata by hours** — spread proportionally to hours worked.
  - **Partner absorbs** — the partner takes the full hit; staff realization is untouched.
  - **Hierarchical cascade** — seniors absorb first, then managers, then staff — the way firms actually behave.
  - **Custom weighted** — assign exact percentages or dollar amounts per person.
- **Impact preview before you commit** — see exactly how each timekeeper's realization changes before the adjustment is saved.
- **Write-ups, not just write-downs** — raise realization symmetrically when an engagement beats plan.
- **Approval thresholds** — adjustments over a firm-set amount route for approval automatically.
- **Full audit trail** — every adjustment and allocation is recorded, with before-and-after values.

**Why firms care:** this is the difference between knowing your firm's average realization and knowing _each person's_ true realization. Performance conversations, comp decisions, and repricing all get sharper when the math reflects what really happened.
