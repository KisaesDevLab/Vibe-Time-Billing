# Administration and security that respects the data you hold

You hold sensitive financial data for every client you serve. Vibe PM is built to protect it, prove what happened to it, and let you configure the firm to your own structure.

- **Role-based access** — partner, manager, senior, staff, and admin role templates, with a permission matrix you can tune.
- **Multi-office** — model your offices, each with its own settings and overrides.
- **Configurable taxonomies** — service lines, work codes, engagement types, and reason codes drive every report dimension.
- **Branding and templates** — your logo and colors propagate across the app, portal, invoices, and emails; customize email and SMS templates with a variable picker.
- **Immutable audit log** — every change is recorded in an append-only log the application can't alter or delete, with anomaly detection for unusual activity.
- **Step-up authentication** — sensitive actions re-verify identity with a second factor, on a firm-set timeout.
- **Strong second factors** — passkeys, authenticator apps, and email or SMS codes for staff sign-in.
- **Encryption at rest** — sensitive data is encrypted with a key your firm controls.
- **Scheduled backups** — automated database backups on your schedule, to your destination, with a documented restore path.

**Why firms care:** data sovereignty isn't a slogan here — it's an architecture. You can see exactly who did what, lock down who can do it, and keep the keys yourself.
