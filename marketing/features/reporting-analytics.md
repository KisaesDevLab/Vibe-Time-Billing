# The numbers partners actually ask for

Every metric that matters to a partner, sliceable by every dimension that matters — and you can always drill from the headline number down to the time entries behind it.

- **Realization** — billed against standard value, after adjustments, so you see what you actually kept.
- **Collection realization** — cash collected against billed.
- **Effective rate** — billed per hour, the true rate you're realizing.
- **Utilization** — billable hours against available hours, by person or role.
- **Profitability** — revenue against loaded cost.
- **Recurring revenue** — monthly and annual recurring revenue, with expansion and churn.
- **Slice every metric** — by timekeeper, client, partner, service line, engagement, fee structure, and period.
- **Drill-through** — go from a summary number to the detail to the underlying entries without leaving the report.
- **WIP and AR aging** — unbilled work and outstanding receivables, bucketed by age.
- **Saved reports** — save the views your firm runs every month and re-run them in a click.
- **Exports** — pull any report to Excel or CSV.

**Why firms care:** decisions about pricing, staffing, and partner comp are only as good as the numbers behind them. Vibe PM gives you firm-wide and per-person clarity without exporting to a spreadsheet to get it.
