# Seven fee structures, no workarounds

Your firm doesn't bill one way, and your software shouldn't force it to. Vibe PM supports seven fee structures natively — pick the right one per engagement and the billing engine handles the rest.

- **Hourly** — straightforward billable time at resolved rates.
- **Hourly with a not-to-exceed cap** — bill by the hour but never above an agreed ceiling, per engagement or per period, with cap checks at billing time.
- **Fixed-fee** — a flat engagement amount, billed your way.
- **Fixed-fee with milestones** — tie billing to deliverables, triggered by date, event, or manually.
- **Recurring subscription** — flat fees on a schedule from weekly to annual, with proration for mid-cycle starts and plan changes.
- **Mixed-mode (retainer + overage)** — bundle included scope into a retainer and bill overage hours on the same invoice.
- **Hour bank** — sell prepaid hours that draw down as work is logged, with rollover caps, expiration, and automatic or manual top-ups.

**Why firms care:** every engagement gets the structure that actually fits it — so your invoices match the deal you made, and your realization reporting stays honest across all of them.
