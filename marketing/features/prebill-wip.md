# Pre-bill and WIP review the way partners want it

Before an invoice goes out, the billing partner gets a clean staging surface to decide what bills, what waits, and what gets written off — engagement by engagement, entry by entry.

- **Billing batches per engagement, per period** — unbilled time is pulled together into one reviewable batch.
- **Include, defer, or write off — per entry** — make the call line by line; deferred time rolls forward into the next period automatically.
- **WIP aging** — see unbilled work bucketed by age (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+) so nothing sits forgotten.
- **Budget comparison** — review WIP against the engagement's budget and fee before you bill.
- **Pre-bill PDFs** — generate a clean pre-bill for partner review and sign-off.
- **Write-off types** — distinguish held write-offs (still counted toward utilization) from removed ones.
- **Carry-forward** — deferred entries reappear in the next period's pre-bill, in context.

**Why firms care:** the pre-bill is where realization is won or lost. Vibe PM makes the partner's review fast and deliberate instead of a spreadsheet scramble at month-end.
