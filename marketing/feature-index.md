# Full feature list

Everything Vibe Practice Management does today, grouped by area. Use this as a navigation source, a sitemap, or a complete "what's included" page.

## Time tracking

- Day, week, and month entry grids with billable/non-billable totals
- Quick entry by engagement, work code, hours, and description
- Description templates per work code
- Billable and in-scope tagging
- Late-entry alerts and firm-wide lockouts
- Entry locking once billed
- Transfer entries between engagements, with audit trail
- Firm-defined required-field rules

## Fee structures

- Hourly
- Hourly with not-to-exceed cap
- Fixed-fee
- Fixed-fee with milestones (date, event, or manual triggers)
- Recurring subscription (weekly through annual) with proration
- Mixed-mode: retainer plus overage
- Hour bank: prepaid hours with rollover, expiration, and top-ups

## Pre-bill & WIP

- Billing batches per engagement, per period
- Include, defer, or write off per entry
- WIP aging buckets (0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+)
- Budget and fee comparison
- Pre-bill PDFs for partner review
- Held vs. removed write-offs
- Automatic carry-forward of deferred time

## Adjustments & realization

- Three adjustment methods: rate, time, fee
- Six allocation methods: specific entries, pro-rata by value, pro-rata by hours, partner absorbs, hierarchical cascade, custom weighted
- Per-timekeeper allocation grain
- Impact preview before commit
- Symmetric write-ups
- Approval thresholds
- Full before/after audit trail

## Invoicing

- Mixed line items (time, fixed, milestone, recurring, expense, processing fee, surcharge, custom)
- Consolidated or separate per client
- Custom numbering schemes
- Adjustable detail level
- Branded PDFs
- Email delivery and one-click resend
- Portal-based read receipts (no tracking pixels)
- Pay-to-unlock attachments
- Voids and credit memos

## Payments & accounts receivable

- Bring-your-own Stripe account
- Card and ACH, with tokenized card data
- Auto-apply payments
- Partial payments and refunds
- Stripe Connect onboarding
- Nightly AR aging snapshots
- Automated, escalating dunning with pay links
- Client statements
- Collection metrics (DSO, collection rate, average days to pay)

## Client portal

- Identity-based access: one person, multiple client entities, with an entity switcher
- Passwordless login by email magic link or SMS code
- View and pay invoices; saved payment methods reused across entities
- Encrypted engagement-level messaging
- In-app document requests
- Files, including pay-to-unlock deliverables
- Per-client notification preferences (email or SMS)
- Firm branding and your own domain
- Mobile-ready

## Proposals & engagement lifecycle

- Proposal editor with rich text, pricing tables, and embedded video
- Selectable pricing packages
- In-portal digital signature capture
- Recurring-revenue rollup on proposals
- Renewal uplift pricing
- Engagement templates
- Lifecycle states (proposed, active, paused, closed)
- Multi-step rollforward wizard

## Scheduling & booking

- Public booking links with a guided client flow
- Configurable appointment types and locations
- Staff availability controls
- Two-way Google and Outlook calendar sync
- Conflict detection
- Automated reminders

## Tax returns & e-signatures

- Tax-return and section tracking
- Tax-payment catalog
- Secure, watermarked client delivery (full or scoped)
- Send documents for signature
- Drag-and-drop field placement
- Multi-signer support
- Signature audit trail

## Documents & files

- Private vs. client-visible control
- Folder templates for new clients
- One-time-code protected share links
- Pay-to-unlock deliverables that release on payment
- In-app document collection
- Appliance or connected cloud storage

## Reporting & analytics

- Realization and collection realization
- Effective rate
- Utilization
- Profitability
- Recurring revenue (MRR/ARR, expansion, churn)
- Slice by timekeeper, client, partner, service line, engagement, fee structure, period
- Drill-through to underlying entries
- WIP and AR aging
- Saved reports
- Excel and CSV export

## AI & automation

- Time-entry description suggestions
- Scope-creep alerts on retainers
- Engagement pricing suggestions
- Local-first AI by default; optional opt-in cloud provider
- Monthly AI budget cap with warning and hard stop
- MCP server with scoped, token-based, audited agent access

## Administration & security

- Role-based access with role templates and a permission matrix
- Multi-office structure with overrides
- Configurable taxonomies (service lines, work codes, engagement types, reason codes)
- Branding across app, portal, invoices, and emails
- Customizable email and SMS templates
- Immutable audit log with anomaly detection
- Step-up authentication for sensitive actions
- Passkey, authenticator, and email/SMS second factors
- Encryption at rest with a firm-controlled key
- Scheduled backups and documented restore

## Deployment & licensing

- Self-hosted Docker appliance (Intel and ARM)
- Three deployment modes: public domain, local network, private mesh
- One flat annual license — unlimited users and clients
- Firm-owned data, payment processor, and domain
