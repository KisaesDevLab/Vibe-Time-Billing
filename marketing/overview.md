# Practice management that models how your firm actually bills

Vibe Practice Management is self-hosted practice management for CPA firms — time tracking, recurring billing, proposals, tax-return tracking, and a branded client portal, all running on hardware you control. One annual license covers unlimited users and unlimited clients. No per-seat pricing, no data leaving your infrastructure unless you choose to send it.

## Who it's for

Built for firms that have outgrown spreadsheets and generic invoicing tools but don't want to hand their client data — or their pricing — to a SaaS vendor. It fits the practice that bills across several fee models at once, runs write-downs through partner review, and wants every staff member's realization to reflect what actually happened on the engagement.

## Why firms choose it

- **You own your data.** A Docker appliance on your own hardware. No mandatory cloud, no vendor lock on your client records, no third party holding your books.
- **One flat annual license.** Unlimited timekeepers, unlimited clients. Add staff or take on clients without watching a per-seat meter.
- **Seven fee structures, natively.** Hourly, hourly with a not-to-exceed cap, fixed-fee, fixed-fee with milestones, recurring subscription, mixed-mode retainer-plus-overage, and prepaid hour banks — no workarounds.
- **Realization that survives down to the individual timekeeper.** When a partner writes down an engagement, six allocation methods decide exactly whose realization absorbs it — so one write-down doesn't unfairly drag everyone's numbers.
- **A client portal your clients will actually use.** One person, one login — even when they manage several entities at your firm. Sign in by email or text, switch between clients, view invoices, pay, message you, and upload documents.
- **You own the payment relationship.** Connect your own Stripe account. Card and ACH, auto-apply, partial payments, refunds — the money moves through your merchant account, not ours.
- **Proposals to signed engagement, in one place.** Build a branded proposal with pricing tables and video, capture a digital signature, and roll it straight into a live engagement.
- **The numbers partners ask for.** Realization, utilization, profitability, and recurring-revenue reporting you can slice by timekeeper, client, partner, service line, engagement, and period — then drill all the way down to the underlying time entries.
- **AI that stays on your server.** Description suggestions, scope-creep detection, and pricing guidance run on a local model by default. Cloud providers are optional and opt-in, with a monthly budget cap.
- **Built for automation.** A built-in MCP server lets AI agents read your data and take scoped, audited actions — useful for the firms building their own AI workflows.
- **Run it your way.** Three deployment modes — public domain, local network, or private mesh — so the appliance fits your security posture, not the other way around.

## What it is — and isn't

Vibe PM covers the engagement-to-cash workflow end to end: time tracking, billing and realization, adjustments, proposals, retainers, tax-return tracking, secure messaging, document collection, and the client portal. It is not a bookkeeping ledger, a payroll system, or a tax-prep engine — it focuses on doing practice management and billing exceptionally well.
